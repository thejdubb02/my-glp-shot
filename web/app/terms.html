What My GLP Shot is

My GLP Shot is a free, personal tracking tool to help you log your own GLP-1 / peptide injections, weight, and related notes. It runs in your browser and stores your data locally on your device. Cloud Sync, if you enable it, stores an end-to-end encrypted blob of your data on our server so you can restore it on another device.

Not medical advice

My GLP Shot is not a medical device, not a diagnostic tool, and not a source of medical advice. The numbers and charts in this app — including the "estimated medication level" chart — are illustrative only, based on a simplified exponential-decay model with a configurable half-life. They are not personalized to your physiology, your prescription, or any clinical pharmacokinetic data. Always follow the instructions of a licensed healthcare provider for your medication. Do not change dosing based on anything you see in this app.

You are responsible for your data

  • The accuracy of what you log is your responsibility.
  • Your local data lives in your browser. If you clear browser storage, uninstall the app, or replace your device without backing up, your local data is gone.
  • If you enable Cloud Sync, your passphrase is the only key to your cloud data. If you lose your passphrase, your cloud copy cannot be recovered. We cannot reset it because we never had it. Save your passphrase in a password manager.
