          Backup

          Your data is stored only on this device. Export regularly. Installing as a PWA dramatically reduces the chance of losing data on a browser clear.

          Switching from another tracker? In your old app, look for an Export Data option (CSV). Then tap "Import" above and select the file. Shots, weight, sites, doses, and notes will transfer.
