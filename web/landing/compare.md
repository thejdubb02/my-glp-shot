# Best GLP-1 tracker apps compared (2026)

> This page compares seven GLP-1 medication trackers: My GLP Shot, Shotsy, CareClinic, Glapp, Glippy, Pep, and MeAgain. Competitor claims are taken from each app's own App Store listing, Google Play listing, or marketing site as of May 5, 2026. My GLP Shot's premium price on this page is $19.99 per year flat. The publisher says it is biased toward its own product and that some rivals are genuinely better for some people. This grid is not medical advice and not a ranking of which drug or dose to use.

## Facts

- Apps in the grid: My GLP Shot, Shotsy, CareClinic, Glapp, Glippy, Pep, MeAgain. MyFitnessPal is not in this grid.
- Last updated on the HTML page: May 5, 2026. Sources: each app's public store listing and marketing site. Corrections: hello@myglpshot.com.
- "Not documented" means the publisher could not find an authoritative public source as of May 2026. It does not mean the feature was proven absent.
- My GLP Shot, on this page: free tier for daily tracking, premium $19.99 per year flat, no account required, local-first, optional end-to-end encrypted sync, open source on GitHub, iOS / Android / web. Mixing calculator (vial, bacteriostatic water, dose, syringe-aware), configurable half-life level chart, and custom dosing (microdose, split-dose, bi-weekly) are described as on the product. Lab tracking, body measurements, doctor-share (encrypted 24-hour link plus PDF), and supply / vial inventory with cost-per-shot are premium.
- My GLP Shot does not, on this page: Apple Watch app, built-in community, food or nutrition tracking (deliberate), telehealth or prescription bundle.
- Prices stated for other apps on this page, not verified again on 2026-09-21: Pep, free download with in-app purchase at $39.99 per year or $9.99 to $39.99 per month. MeAgain, about $10 per month for the app (about $120 per year) or about $30 per month for Care (about $360 or more per year with Care). Shotsy and CareClinic are listed only as "Subscription", with no dollar amount. Glapp and Glippy have no paid tier.
- Encryption claim on this page: My GLP Shot derives an AES-256-GCM key in the browser with PBKDF2-SHA-256, 600,000 iterations. The server stores opaque ciphertext. The page says none of the other six apps document end-to-end encryption.
- Where this page says a rival is the better fit: Glippy for a moderated peer community (free). Glapp for native iOS polish and Apple Watch (free, iOS only). Pep for food and macro tracking (subscription for full features). MeAgain for telehealth and prescriptions bundled with the tracker. Shotsy for a polished native paid app with a loyal user base. CareClinic for symptom and chronic-illness tracking wider than GLP-1.

## At a glance

- My GLP Shot: Free, plus $19.99 per year flat. Local-first, optional end-to-end encrypted multi-device sync, mixing calculator, level chart, custom dosing schedules, open source. App: https://app.myglpshot.com/
- Shotsy: Subscription. Polished iOS-first tracker with a following in the GLP-1 community. Native app, paid premium tier.
- CareClinic: Free plus a paid tier. General health and symptom tracker. GLP-1 logging is one of many uses.
- Glapp: Free, iOS only, as of May 2026. Apple Watch, Vision Pro, iPhone widgets, medication-level curves, supply tracking. No paid tier as of May 2026.
- Glippy: Free. iOS and Android. Moderated peer community. Daily check-ins, HealthKit sync, PDF and CSV export. Email-only signup.
- Pep: Free download, $9.99 to $39.99 per month in-app purchase. Positioned as a MyFitnessPal companion. Nutrition tracking (fiber, protein, calories, water) plus basic shot logging.
- MeAgain: About $10 per month for the app, about $30 per month for Care. Tracking bundled with telehealth and prescription fulfillment. Subscription required for the tracker.

## What this page says the decision hinges on

Privacy. My GLP Shot defaults to local-only storage in the browser. Data reaches a server only if the person opts into multi-device sync. That sync is end-to-end encrypted as described in Facts. Source: https://github.com/thejdubb02/my-glp-shot. This page says none of the other apps document end-to-end encryption. It calls Glippy the next most privacy-conscious (email-only signup, and an App Store privacy card that marks usage and diagnostics as not linked to you) but still cloud-based. It says the other apps store data in a form the operator can read.

Pricing. Glapp and Glippy have no paid tier. My GLP Shot has a usable free tier and is the one in this grid with a $19.99 per year tier for clinical features such as lab tracking and the doctor-share PDF. The page says other subscription apps in the category cluster around $5 to $10 per month, which is $60 to $120 per year, versus $19.99.

Medication-side depth. This page names three tools as not commodity features: the mixing calculator, custom dosing schedules, and the active-medication concentration chart. It says My GLP Shot ships all three on the free tier, Glapp ships the level chart but not the others, and the remaining apps focus on simpler shot logs and weight tracking.

Cross-platform. For a household with both an iPhone and an Android phone, this page lists My GLP Shot (a progressive web app in the browser), CareClinic, Glippy, Pep, MeAgain, and Shotsy. Glapp is iOS-only.
