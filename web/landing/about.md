# About My GLP Shot

> My GLP Shot is a GLP-1 medication tracking app for people taking semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy) or tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound). It logs doses, injection sites, weight, side effects, and lab numbers, and keeps that record on the device. It does not sell, prescribe, supply, or source GLP-1 drugs. It is not a pharmacy, a compounding pharmacy, or a telehealth service. Nothing in the app is medical advice. The prescriber decides the dose. Page last updated August 1, 2026.

## How it works, as this page describes it

1. Open https://app.myglpshot.com in a browser. Install it to the home screen if you want an icon.
2. Log each shot with its dose and injection site. The app rotates sites and warns when a site is repeated too soon.
3. Add weight, side effects, mood, or lab numbers when you have them.
4. Watch the dose timeline and the estimated medication level so a plateau or a missed week is visible.
5. Export a doctor-ready PDF, or a 24-hour read-only link, before an appointment.

No account is required for the free tier.
