Frequently asked questions

What GLP-1 medications can I track?

My GLP Shot supports the common GLP-1 weight-loss and diabetes shots: Tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound), Semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy), Liraglutide (Saxenda, Victoza), Dulaglutide (Trulicity), and compounded or reconstituted peptides. You can add a custom medication name and half-life if your shot is not in the preset list.

Is my data really private?

Yes. All shot logs, weight entries, and notes are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB on your device. If you enable optional Cloud Sync, your data is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your password (PBKDF2, 600,000 iterations) before it leaves the device. The server stores opaque ciphertext only.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. Open the app and start tracking shots immediately, with no email and no signup. An account is only needed for Cloud Sync or Premium features.

How does cloud sync work?

Cloud Sync is end-to-end encrypted. Your data is encrypted in the browser before upload. The server sees an anonymous lookup ID, ciphertext, and a timestamp, and it never sees your username, password, encryption key, or any health data. If you lose your passphrase the cloud copy cannot be recovered.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Settings > Backup exports your full shot history, weights, and notes as a JSON file. You can also delete all local data or the encrypted cloud blob at any time.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. My GLP Shot is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that runs in any modern browser on iPhone, Android, iPad, Mac, and Windows. Install it to your home screen for an app-like experience and use it offline. No App Store or Google Play download required.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first load, My GLP Shot works fully offline. Log shots, view your calendar, check weight trends, and use the mixing calculator without internet.

How is this different from Shotsy?

My GLP Shot is local-by-default (your data stays on your device unless you opt into encrypted sync), runs on phone, tablet, and computer instead of phone-only, is open source so anyone can audit the code, includes a free reconstitution calculator and active-medication-level chart, and costs $19.99 per year, roughly 60% less than other popular GLP-1 trackers.

Is this medical advice?

No. My GLP Shot is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device and not a source of medical advice. The estimated medication-level chart uses a simplified exponential-decay model and is illustrative only. Always follow your licensed healthcare provider's instructions.

Can I share with my doctor?

Yes. Premium includes a 90-day PDF report and a one-tap private share link that expires after 24 hours and gives your provider read-only access to your recent log.

What does it cost?

Free forever for shot logging, calendar, weight tracking, mixing calculator, active-medication chart, reminders, and single-device backup. Premium is $19.99/year or $1.99/month with a 14-day free trial. No credit card to start.

Is the source code public?

Yes. The full source is on GitHub. Anyone can audit the encryption, the local storage layer, and the sync server.
