How My GLP Shot compares

Shotsy is closed source and stores your shot history on its own servers. My GLP Shot is open source, runs as a Progressive Web App (no App Store), keeps data on your device by default, and end-to-end encrypts the optional cloud sync — so even we cannot read it.

vs. CareClinic, MyTherapy, Medisafe

Generic medication trackers are great for pill schedules but were not built for injection therapy. My GLP Shot adds purpose-built features: a body diagram with site-rotation hints, a half-life-based active-medication level chart, dose-titration history, and a mixing calculator for compounded peptides.

vs. Apple Health, Google Fit, Fitbit

Those track steps, weight, and heart rate but not the data that matters for GLP-1 therapy: which site you injected, what dose, when the next shot is due, side-effect severity over time, mood, appetite, or supply runway. My GLP Shot is a focused tool for the GLP-1 journey specifically.

vs. spreadsheet or paper journal

A spreadsheet works, until you want a chart of how much medication is in your system right now, or a one-tap reminder that you missed a shot, or a doctor-share link for your next appointment. My GLP Shot gives you all of that without giving up the privacy of a private journal.

Frequently asked questions

What GLP-1 medications can I track?

My GLP Shot supports the common GLP-1 weight-loss and diabetes shots: Tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound), Semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy), Liraglutide (Saxenda, Victoza), Dulaglutide (Trulicity), and compounded or reconstituted peptides. You can add a custom medication name and half-life if your shot is not in the preset list.

Is my data really private?

Yes. All shot logs, weight entries, and notes are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB on your device. If you enable optional Cloud Sync, your data is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your password (PBKDF2, 600,000 iterations) before it leaves the device. The server stores opaque ciphertext only.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. Open the app and start tracking shots immediately, with no email and no signup. An account is only needed for Cloud Sync or Premium features.

How does cloud sync work?

Cloud Sync is end-to-end encrypted. Your data is encrypted in the browser before upload. The server sees an anonymous lookup ID, ciphertext, and a timestamp, and it never sees your username, password, encryption key, or any health data. If you lose your passphrase the cloud copy cannot be recovered.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Settings > Backup exports your full shot history, weights, and notes as a JSON file. You can also delete all local data or the encrypted cloud blob at any time.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. My GLP Shot is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that runs in any modern browser on iPhone, Android, iPad, Mac, and Windows. Install it to your home screen for an app-like experience and use it offline. No App Store or Google Play download required.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first load, My GLP Shot works fully offline. Log shots, view your calendar, check weight trends, and use the mixing calculator without internet.

How is this different from Shotsy?

My GLP Shot is local-by-default (your data stays on your device unless you opt into encrypted sync), runs on phone, tablet, and computer instead of phone-only, is open source so anyone can audit the code, includes a free reconstitution calculator and active-medication-level chart, and costs $19.99 per year, roughly 60% less than other popular GLP-1 trackers.

Is this medical advice?

No. My GLP Shot is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device and not a source of medical advice. The estimated medication-level chart uses a simplified exponential-decay model and is illustrative only. Always follow your licensed healthcare provider's instructions.

Can I share with my doctor?

Yes. Premium includes a 90-day PDF report and a one-tap private share link that expires after 24 hours and gives your provider read-only access to your recent log.

What does it cost?

Free forever for shot logging, calendar, weight tracking, mixing calculator, active-medication chart, reminders, and single-device backup. Premium is $19.99/year or $1.99/month with a 14-day free trial. No credit card to start.

GLP-1 terms, in plain English

Quick definitions for terms you will see across GLP-1 communities and in My GLP Shot.

GLP-1
Glucagon-like peptide-1. A hormone your gut releases after eating. GLP-1 receptor agonists (the "shots") mimic it to lower blood sugar and reduce appetite. Includes Tirzepatide and Semaglutide.
Tirzepatide
The active ingredient in Mounjaro and Zepbound. A dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist taken as a weekly shot.
Semaglutide
The active ingredient in Ozempic and Wegovy. A GLP-1 receptor agonist taken as a weekly shot, also available as a daily oral tablet (Rybelsus).
Titration
Stepping up your dose gradually to let your body adjust and reduce side effects. Most GLP-1 protocols start low (for example 2.5 mg tirzepatide) and increase every 4 weeks.
Peak and trough
"Peak" is the highest concentration of medication in your blood after a shot. "Trough" is the lowest, right before the next dose. The active-medication chart in the app visualizes both.
Half-life
How long it takes for half of a dose to clear your system. Tirzepatide is about 5 days; semaglutide is about 7 days. That is why both are dosed weekly.
Bioavailability
The fraction of a dose that actually reaches your bloodstream. Subcutaneous GLP-1 shots have high bioavailability; oral semaglutide is much lower, which is why oral doses are bigger.
Microdosing
Splitting a weekly dose into smaller, more frequent shots (for example twice a week). Some users do this to smooth side effects. Talk to your doctor before changing your protocol.
Injection sites
The areas where GLP-1 shots are given subcutaneously: abdomen (avoid 2 inches around the navel), upper outer thigh, and back of the upper arm. Rotate sites to reduce irritation; My GLP Shot suggests the next site for you.
Reconstitution
Mixing a powdered peptide with bacteriostatic water to make an injectable solution. The mixing calculator tells you how many insulin-syringe units equal your prescribed dose.
Plateau
A period (typically 4+ weeks) where weight loss stalls. Common at every dose level. Plateau alerts in My GLP Shot flag this and suggest questions to ask your doctor.
PWA (Progressive Web App)
A website you can install to your home screen like an app. Works offline, sends push notifications, no app-store download.
