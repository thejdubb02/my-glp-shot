  <h2>When to choose My GLP Shot</h2>
  <p>Choose My GLP Shot if your main goal is to understand and manage your GLP-1 medication routine in detail. Its purpose-built features provide insights general wellness apps cannot.</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>You want to see your medication concentration.</strong> The active-medication chart visualizes estimated peak and trough levels of tirzepatide or semaglutide in your system, helping you connect how you feel to where you are in your dose cycle.</li>
    <li><strong>You follow a non-standard schedule.</strong> If you microdose, split-dose, or use a custom interval, My GLP Shot supports those schedules natively. Foundayo can only remind you to take a medication at a fixed time.</li>
    <li><strong>Privacy is non-negotiable.</strong> My GLP Shot’s <a href="/privacy.html">local-first design</a> means your health data never leaves your control unless you explicitly opt in, and even then it's encrypted before it goes anywhere.</li>
    <li><strong>You need a simple, affordable tool.</strong> The free tier covers everything most users need. The one-time annual premium fee is straightforward and significantly lower than many subscription-based wellness apps.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>When to choose Foundayo</h2>
  <p>Choose Foundayo if you are looking for a holistic wellness dashboard that includes medication as just one of many health habits you track.</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>You track multiple wellness pillars.</strong> Foundayo is designed to bring sleep, nutrition, exercise, mindfulness, and medication into a single, cohesive view.</li>
    <li><strong>You prefer native mobile apps.</strong> Foundayo offers dedicated iOS and Android apps, while My GLP Shot is a Progressive Web App that installs from your browser.</li>
    <li><strong>Your GLP-1 tracking needs are basic.</strong> If you simply need a reminder to take your shot and a place to log that you did, Foundayo’s habit-logging can suffice. You won’t get GLP-1-specific analytics, but you may not need them.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Using both apps together</h2>
  <p>It’s common for users to employ both tools. You might use:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>My GLP Shot</strong> as your private, authoritative log for everything related to your GLP-1 medication: exact doses, injection sites, side effects, lab results, and the medication concentration chart.</li>
    <li><strong>Foundayo</strong> as your daily wellness dashboard to track sleep, water intake, general mood, and exercise, seeing how those habits interact with your broader health picture.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>This approach gives you the depth of a specialist tool for your medication and the breadth of a generalist tool for overall wellness. You can learn more about how My GLP Shot fits into a broader health routine on our <a href="/about.html">About page</a>.</p>

  <h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
  <div class="faq">
    <h3>What is Foundayo?</h3>
    <p>Foundayo is a health and wellness app that offers tracking for a variety of habits and metrics. It is not specifically designed for GLP-1 medication tracking. My GLP Shot is purpose-built for the GLP-1 routine, with features like an active-medication concentration chart and dose-cycle-aware appetite trends that Foundayo does not have.</p>

    <h3>Is Foundayo free for GLP-1 tracking?</h3>
    <p>Foundayo offers a free tier for general tracking. For dedicated GLP-1 tracking, My GLP Shot provides a free tier that covers shot logging, weight tracking, reminders, mood and appetite logs, photos, and the active-medication concentration chart. Premium is $19.99 per year flat for advanced features like lab tracking and PDF doctor exports.</p>

    <h3>Which app is better for privacy?</h3>
    <p>My GLP Shot defaults to local-only storage, keeping your data on your device. Optional cloud sync is end-to-end encrypted with AES-256-GCM in your browser before upload. The server stores opaque ciphertext and has no decryption key. Foundayo, as a cloud-based wellness app, stores user data on its servers. Its privacy policy details how that data is handled.</p>

    <h3>Can Foundayo track Mounjaro or Ozempic doses?</h3>
    <p>Foundayo can log a medication as a generic habit or entry, but it lacks GLP-1-specific features. It cannot model medication half-lives, track injection-site rotation, support custom microdosing schedules, or correlate appetite trends to your dose cycle. My GLP Shot is built specifically for these tasks.</p>

    <h3>Do I need both Foundayo and My GLP Shot?</h3>
    <p>If you use Foundayo for general wellness or habit tracking and also take a GLP-1 medication, using both apps is a common approach. Use Foundayo for its broad wellness features and My GLP Shot for the dedicated, in-depth tracking of your GLP-1 medication routine, side effects, and related health metrics.</p>
