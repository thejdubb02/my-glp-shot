What ShotClock is

ShotClock is a free, personal tracking tool to help you log your own GLP-1 / peptide injections, weight, and related notes. It runs in your browser and stores your data locally on your device. Cloud Sync, if you enable it, stores an end-to-end encrypted blob of your data on our server so you can restore it on another device.

Not medical advice

ShotClock is not a medical device, not a diagnostic tool, and not a source of medical advice. The numbers and charts in this app — including the "estimated medication level" chart — are illustrative only, based on a simplified exponential-decay model with a configurable half-life. They are not personalized to your physiology, your prescription, or any clinical pharmacokinetic data. Always follow the instructions of a licensed healthcare provider for your medication. Do not change dosing based on anything you see in this app.

  • The accuracy of what you log is your responsibility.
