**Summary of Blast Radius**

The two **CRITICAL** findings are the primary blockers before shipping to real patients:

1. The raw AES-GCM key is stored in localStorage in a form that any XSS can immediately use to decrypt all health data.
2. The sync logic has multiple paths that can silently discard a user’s medication injection history when they log in on a second device.

These must be fixed before production. The base64 handling bugs are also high-priority as they can lead to permanent decryption failures.
