- [HIGH] api/app.py:470 — Webhook idempotency race condition. Fix: The `SELECT` then `INSERT` pattern for idempotency is vulnerable to a race condition where two concurrent requests could both `SELECT` no existing record, then both attempt to `INSERT`, causing a `UNIQUE` constraint violation for the second. The second event would then not be processed. Use `INSERT OR IGNORE` and then re-`SELECT` to ensure the event is processed by the first successful `INSERT`, or wrap the `INSERT` in a `try-except IntegrityError` block and proceed with processing only if the `INSERT` succeeded.
